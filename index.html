Our story

Born from one simple idea: every neighbourhood needs a living room.

The Common began as a small espresso bar on a quiet corner, opened by two friends who wanted somewhere to linger. No rush, no hustle, only good coffee and warm company.

Today we work with roasters who pay farmers fairly, bake in-house every morning, and keep a seat free for whoever walks in next. We are proudly independent, and we like it that way.

With warmth, the team at The Common

The best cafés don't just serve coffee. They give a street its heartbeat.
